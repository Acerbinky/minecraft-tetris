HOW TO PLAY

← → move   ↑ flip pair   ↓ soft drop   SPACE hard drop   P pause

• Pairs drop together — two linked blocks fall as one piece. Flip it vertical or horizontal and swap which block leads; both blocks land wherever the pair stops, overhangs and all.
• Match 3+ in a row or column to clear.
• Chains count — after a clear, falling blocks can match again for bonus points.
• Tap the board on touch screens to flip the pair; swipe down to slam.
• Speed rises every level. At Level 10 the world flips to the Nether.
